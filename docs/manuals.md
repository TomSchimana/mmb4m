# Manuals

MMB4M has no manual of its own. The language is MMBasic, and its manuals come from the people who write it. The pages here only describe what is different on a Mac.

The manual to read is the PicoMite User Manual. MMBasic for Linux follows it, and MMB4M follows MMBasic for Linux. Geoff Graham publishes it with each release on the [PicoMite page](https://geoffg.net/picomite.html), where it is also a [direct download](https://geoffg.net/Downloads/picomite/PicoMite_User_Manual.pdf). Peter Mather keeps the newest version in the [PDF folder](https://github.com/UKTailwind/PicoMite/tree/main/PDF) of his PicoMite repository, together with manuals for single areas such as `BLIT`, 3D graphics and game controllers. The commands MMB4M 0.2 added come from that newer version.

A program written for a Colour Maximite 2 is described by its manual on the [Colour Maximite 2 page](https://geoffg.net/maximite.html). The manual for its version 6 is part of the [beta download](https://geoffg.net/Downloads/Maximite/CMM2_Beta.zip).

MMBasic for Linux adds commands of its own, for the terminal and the operating system among them. Its [README](https://github.com/thwill1000/mmb4l#readme) gives their full syntax, and [What MMB4M adds](commands.md) says how they behave on a Mac.

Questions about MMBasic itself are best asked on [The Back Shed](https://www.thebackshed.com/forum/ViewForum.php?FID=16), the forum where its authors post.
